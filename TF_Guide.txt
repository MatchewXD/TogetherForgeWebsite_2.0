P5
Badge & Recognition System
0/21 items

Not started
Official definition of badges, Forge Marks, and profile achievement records. Reference this section when implementing recognition on profiles, posts, and donations. Sits after Community Infrastructure (P4) so Get Involved and credit work can point here.


Badges: Design data model for badges, Forge Marks balances, award ledger, and profile achievement records


Forge Marks: Grant Marks only from completed donations at base rate $1 = 100 Marks, with progressive bonus tiers ($1–24: 100/$, $25–49: 110/$, $50–99: 120/$, $100–249: 130/$, $250–499: 140/$, $500+: 150/$)


Forge Marks: Enforce rules — never expire, cannot withdraw as cash, cannot transfer between users


Community Awards: Implement placing Spark (100), Hammer (200), Anvil (500), and Masterwork (1,000) Marks on posts; awards are permanent and cannot be removed by the giver


Community Awards: Optional short custom message on Anvil and Masterwork awards


Profile Achievement Record: Permanently log every received award with type/name, date, link to original post, and running totals by award tier


Status Badges: Active Subscriber · Donor — implement earn conditions and profile display


Donation Milestone Badges: Lifetime totals at $10, $50, $100, $250, $500, $1,000, $2,500, $5,000, $10,000, $25,000, $50,000, $100,000 Donor


Tasks Shipped Badges: First Ship, then 5, 10, 25, 50, 75, 100, 150, 200, 250 Tasks Shipped


Project Contribution Badges: Game Shipper — implement earn condition and display


Starter Badges: First Idea · Showcase · First Feedback · Task Claimed · Early Supporter


Impact Badges (single post): Discussion Starter · Well Received · Deep Discussion · Community Favorite · Awarded Idea


Impact Badges (account-level): Recognized · Respected · Distinguished


Impact Badges (stretch): Talk of the Forge · Viral Idea


Giving & Engagement Volume Badges: First Spark Given · Generous · Patron · Commentator · Active Voice · Supporter · Enthusiast


Collaboration Badges: Joined Force · Shared Victory


Badge icons: Flat geometric minimalist vector style — medium black outlines, warm amber/orange and soft teal accents, clean simple shapes, transparent background, centered, no text; simpler for lower-effort, richer layering for higher-strive


Badges UI: Show earned badges and award history on user profiles; show Marks balance where users can place awards


Badges UI: Award placement flow on posts is clear, shows cost, confirms spend, and updates profile + post immediately


Badges: Wire auto-grant triggers for milestone, starter, task-shipped, and engagement badges; keep system simple and transparent (no hidden multipliers or temporary boosts)


Badges: End-to-end test donation → Marks credit → place award → profile record + post display; verify permanence and edge cases (insufficient Marks, already awarded, etc.)
